FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before getting tattooed or pierced at Heartbreakers Tattoo Shop.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome at Heartbreakers Tattoo Shop. Availability can vary depending on the artist and the complexity of your tattoo, so contacting us ahead of time is recommended for larger or custom pieces.

Do I need an appointment for a tattoo?

Appointments are recommended for custom tattoos, larger pieces, sleeves, and projects that require dedicated design time. Walk-ins are also welcome when an artist has availability.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Tattoo pricing depends on the artist, size, placement, style, detail, and estimated session time. For an accurate quote, send us your idea, reference images, placement, approximate size, and preferred artist.

Can I bring my own tattoo idea?

Absolutely. Your ideas, reference images, sketches, and personal story can all help your artist create a custom piece. Your artist can also help refine the concept, composition, placement, and overall design.

Do you offer cover-ups?

Yes. Cover-up work is available. Because every existing tattoo is different, we recommend sending a clear photo of the tattoo along with your desired concept so an artist can determine what approach will work best.

Do you offer professional body piercing?

Yes. Heartbreakers offers professional body piercing, including personalized placement and jewelry guidance. Contact the shop for current piercing availability and jewelry options.

What should I bring to my tattoo appointment?

Bring your photo ID, reference images or ideas, and wear comfortable clothing that provides easy access to the tattoo area. Make sure you have eaten and are properly hydrated before your appointment.

How do I book an appointment?

You can contact Heartbreakers through Instagram, call the shop, or stop by in person. For custom work, include as much information as possible about your idea, placement, size, and preferred artist.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, we do! We offer financing options to help make your purchase more affordable. You can apply through our financing partner and see what options you qualify for.
