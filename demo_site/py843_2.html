Client Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our in-home chef service.

Are grocery costs included in the weekly rate?

No. Grocery costs are kept separate so you only pay for the exact ingredients sourced for your household without markup. Receipts are provided weekly alongside your service invoice.

Do I need to be home while Chef Josh is cooking?

Not at all. Many of our executive clients provide secure entry access. Chef Josh completes the preparation, packages all food with clear instructions, cleans the kitchen thoroughly, and locks up upon departure.

Can dietary preferences or allergies be accommodated?

Yes. We routinely accommodate Gluten-Free, Keto, Paleo, Low-Sodium, Dairy-Free, and specific allergy profiles. All preferences are detailed during our initial consultation.

What geographic areas in South Carolina are served?

We primary serve Beaufort, Bluffton, Hilton Head Island, Okatie, and surrounding Lowcountry South Carolina communities.
