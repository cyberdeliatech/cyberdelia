Soy Yo

The Story

Telling His Own Stories

Alam A’s journey didn't start with a microphone; it started behind the turntables. At just 15 years old, he was already making a name for himself as a DJ, studying the rhythms that moved crowds and the lyrics that touched hearts.

That deep understanding of musical structure naturally evolved into songwriting. For years, other artists brought Alam's compositions to life. But as the stories became more personal, he realized that nobody could convey the genuine melancholy and romanticism of his words quite like he could.

He chose bachata—not just for its commercial appeal, but because its inherently emotional and melancholic character perfectly mirrored his artistic soul. Drawn to the modern styles of Romeo Santos and Prince Royce, Alam A carved his own lane.

"The breakout single 'Enamorado' captured attention across the U.S. and Dominican Republic, peaking at #27 on Billboard, aided by the legendary production of Aventura's Lenny Santos."

Now, with his highly anticipated 13-track debut album It's Me (Soy Yo), Alam A steps out of the shadows and fully into the spotlight.
