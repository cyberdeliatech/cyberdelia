Our National Board-Certified Family Nurse Practitioner holds a Master of Science in Nursing from the University of Miami and brings extensive experience across primary care, men’s and women’s health, aesthetics, wellness, and dermatology. Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

Neurotoxins (like Botox and Dysport) generally last between 3 to 4 months. Dermal fillers vary depending on the area treated and product used, typically lasting anywhere from 6 to 18 months.
Most treatments have minimal to zero downtime. You may experience slight redness or minor swelling at the injection sites, which typically subsides within 24 to 48 hours.
We recommend arriving with a clean face free of heavy makeup if possible. Avoid blood-thinning supplements (like aspirin or ibuprofen) for 48 hours prior if approved by your physician, to minimize bruising.
